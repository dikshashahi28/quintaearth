Sustainable Development Goals

Seventeen goals. One shared map.

In 2015 the nations of the UN set down seventeen goals — a shared map through 2030 for people and the planet. Poverty, hunger, water, energy, climate, the living commons, and the work of keeping them together.

QuintaEarth sits with that map. Same ground as greentech and cleantech. The names below are the UN’s. The lines are ours. Each goal opens onto the official pages if you want the source.
