In early 2026, **Torque Agency Group** (automotive and manufacturing communications) and Midlands sustainability consultancy **Wylde Connections** announced a partnership aimed at a familiar double bind: businesses that over-claim and get punished, and businesses that under-claim — *greenhushing* — because UK enforcement risk feels sharper than storytelling reward. Public materials cite the UK Competition and Markets Authority’s stronger toolkit on misleading environmental claims and position the joint offer around evidence-led ESG diagnostics (including Wylde’s Enveglas tool) paired with defensible communications.

The industry signal is structural, not glamorous. Strategy and narrative are being forced into the same workshop. That mirrors what EmpCo is doing on the EU consumer side: communications that cannot survive scrutiny are becoming liabilities.

QuintaEarth notes the pattern without adopting either firm’s marketing: the market is paying for *proof architecture* — diagnostics, roadmaps, scoped claims — alongside media craft. Sustainability here is something teams *build together*, not a seasonal press stunt. This desk note summarises public partnership reporting; it is not an endorsement and invents no client results.
