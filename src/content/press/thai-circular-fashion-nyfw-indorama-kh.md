In mid-September 2026, Thai creative streetwear label **KH Editions** presented work at New York Fashion Week with material support from **Indorama Ventures**: fabrics woven with the company’s **deja** recycled polyester yarn, drawn from recycled PET. Company releases describe two looks and accessories within a ten-look Spring/Summer 2027 collection, shown in New York on **15 September 2026**, building on Indorama’s long-running **RECO** designer programme.

The story that matters for makers is not the zip code of the show. It is the supply-chain handshake: chemical recycling know-how meeting a designer who treats waste streams as raw material for identity, not as a apology sticker. Thailand’s trade promotion channels reportedly helped stage the international push — a reminder that SEA soft power in fashion increasingly travels through *circular competence*, not only through exoticised craft narratives.

QuintaEarth’s reading: when recycled PET appears on a global calendar as design-forward streetwear, sustainability stops being a donation narrative and becomes industrial culture — yarn, weave, cut, show. EmpCo-era Europe will ask the next question aloud: can the claim on the hangtag survive substantiation? The makers who answer with materials systems, not slogans, will keep the stage.
