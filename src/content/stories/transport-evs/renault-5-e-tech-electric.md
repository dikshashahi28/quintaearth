## The problem

Passenger cars produce around 16% of the EU's CO2 emissions ([European Commission](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/transport-decarbonisation/road-transport/cars-and-vans_en)). The biggest-selling cars in Europe are small hatchbacks, used mainly for short and medium trips, which are ideal for batteries. Yet for years small EVs were scarce: batteries were expensive, margins were thin and most European carmakers focused on larger, pricier electric SUVs.

That left buyers with a choice between a cheap petrol car and a costly EV. Without affordable small electric models made in Europe, both the climate targets and Europe's car industry were at risk, as cheaper Chinese EVs entered the market.

## The product

The **Renault 5 E-Tech electric** is a 4-metre-class hatchback whose design echoes the 1972 Renault 5. It is built on a dedicated small-car electric platform and assembled at Douai, part of Renault's ElectriCity industrial hub in northern France. Following an update announced on 25 August 2026 ([Renault](https://media.renault.com/the-renault-5-e-tech-electric-range-gains-fresh-appeal-with-greater-efficiency-range-and-versatility-right-from-entry-level-along-with-new-colours-and-features/?lang=eng)):

- the 40 kWh Urban Range battery with a 90 kW (120 hp) motor gives up to 315 km WLTP;
- the 52 kWh Comfort Range battery with a 110 kW (150 hp) motor gives up to 430 km WLTP, up from 410 km;
- a new entry-level Five version swaps to an 80 kW motor and a 36.5 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery with cell-to-pack design, for up to 305 km;
- the new Five gets an 80 kW DC fast charger as standard, alongside a 6.6 kW AC charger;
- Google built-in, with the Gemini assistant arriving by an optional over-the-air update.

In Spain, the cheapest R5 starts at about EUR 23,056 before subsidies, below an equivalent petrol Clio, according to ActualidadMotor.

The R5 matters inside Renault because it carries the group's electric strategy in the volume segment. Renault Group reports that the Renault brand was number two in retail EV sales in Europe in H1 2026, and that the "success of electric vehicles" was the main reason for a positive product mix. The Alpine A290, a sportier car on the same base, shares its Car of the Year title.

## How it works

1. **Charge:** most owners charge at home or work on AC power, overnight or during the day.
2. **Drive:** a front-mounted electric motor drives the front wheels, with no gearbox.
3. **Recover:** regenerative braking recharges the battery in town traffic.
4. **Fast charge:** on longer trips the car takes DC fast charging at public stations.
5. **Manage:** a "Smart" driving mode switches between Eco, Comfort and Sport automatically.
6. **Update:** software and assistant features are added over the air.

## Impact and numbers

- **Sales:** the Renault 5 recorded 45,629 European registrations in the first half of 2026, making it the fourth best-selling electric car in Europe and the leading B-segment EV ([ActualidadMotor](https://en.actualidadmotor.com/Renault-5-Electric-boosts-Renault%27s-profits-in-Europe/)).
- **Group effect:** Renault Group's EV sales in Europe rose 47.6% in H1 2026 to 18.8% of its sales, and electrified models reached 52%. Revenue rose 9.5% to EUR 30.3 billion, and the group said the success of electric vehicles drove a positive product mix ([Renault Group](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/29/3335491/0/en/Renault-Group-delivers-robust-performance-confirms-its-2026-FY-guidance-and-rapidly-implements-futuREady.html)).
- **Jobs:** to meet demand, Douai has returned to a three-shift pattern, recruited about 350 more temporary workers for the night shift after 550 since October 2025, and aims for around 1,200 and up to 1,400 vehicles a day ([Automobile Propre](https://www.automobile-propre.com/articles/bonne-nouvelle-pour-lemploi-la-r5-electrique-fait-tourner-les-chaines-a-plein-regime-a-douai/)).
- **Recognition:** 60 jurors from across Europe chose the R5 and Alpine A290 as Car of the Year 2025 ahead of the Kia EV3 and five other finalists ([Car of the Year](https://caroftheyear.org/news/renault-5-alpine-a290-the-new-car-of-the-year-2025/)).

**Honest caveats.** WLTP ranges are higher than most drivers will see on motorways or in winter. The car's life-cycle footprint depends on how its battery cells are made and on the electricity that charges it; Renault publishes no R5-specific life-cycle figure in these sources. In many countries the R5 still costs more than a comparable petrol car without subsidies, and Renault itself reports price pressure in Europe and higher regulatory costs. The automotive operating margin fell to 3.0% in H1 2026, showing that small EVs remain hard to make profitable. Long delivery times were one reason for the Douai ramp-up. Policy is shifting too: in December 2025 the Commission proposed replacing the 2035 zero-emission target for new cars with a 90% CO2 cut, allowing plug-in hybrids and some combustion cars after 2035 ([European Commission](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/transport-decarbonisation/road-transport/cars-and-vans_en)); the European Parliament's transport committee postponed its vote in October 2026 ([electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/10/02/eu-transport-committee-postpones-auto-package-vote/)).

## What's next

The LFP Five version, with cheaper and more durable battery chemistry, is the key to bringing prices down further. The updated range goes on show at the Paris Motor Show from 12 to 18 October 2026. Renault is following the R5 with the Twingo E-Tech electric, launched in Europe in the first half of 2026.

## Why it matters for Europe / green buyers

The R5 shows that a European-designed, European-built small EV can sell in large numbers and lift a carmaker's results. The Commission's proposal would add "super credits" for small affordable EVs made in the EU before 2035, which would reward exactly this kind of car. For fleet and private buyers, the R5 is a benchmark: a city car with over 300 km of range, fast charging and a price close to petrol rivals.

India's electric car market is growing fast but from a small base: even the market leader, Tata Motors, retailed 78,848 electric cars in FY 2025-26, about 40% of the segment ([Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/analysis-sales/tata-ev-sales-cross-300000-units-hit-a-new-high-in-march-and-fy2026-131982)). Renault's sales in India rose 61% in H1 2026, but the R5 is a European product. Its lesson for India is that small, desirable, locally built EVs with cheaper LFP batteries are what move the mass market.
