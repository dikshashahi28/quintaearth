Automotive comms specialist **Torque Agency Group** and sustainability consultancy **Wylde Connections** publicly framed their 2026 partnership around a dual failure mode: greenwash (claims ahead of evidence) and greenhush (silence even when progress is real), especially under sharper UK enforcement talk around misleading environmental claims.

The structural insight for brands and their agencies is simple. Narrative teams without ESG diagnostics invent risk; ESG teams without narrative craft leave good work invisible. An integrated desk — diagnostic tool plus communications discipline — is not a trend story; it is how EmpCo-era and CMA-era markets will buy services.

For Europe ↔ SEA clients, translate carefully: local enforcement cultures differ, but the craft standard converges. Build the evidence file first. Write the line second. Measure the distance between them every quarter. QuintaEarth’s own posture matches that order — commentary on what is made, never theatre that outruns the workshop.

No client results are claimed here; the sources are the firms’ own partnership announcements and regional business press.
