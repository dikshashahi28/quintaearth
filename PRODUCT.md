# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences with equal weight (confirmed 8 Oct 2026):

- **Phase 1 buyers.** Indian exporters selling to Europe, European buyers and brands sourcing from India, and CSR teams and foundations in India. Their job: make sustainability claims they can back with evidence before EmpCo, CBAM and CSRD scrutiny reaches them. Internal split from the vault (5 Oct): about 50% exporters, 40% European buyers/brands, 10% India CSR.
- **Community and makers.** Greentech and cleantech startups, companies, makers, scholars and volunteers. Their job: learn a field, find and be found by peers, show their work, and follow one industry or sub-category over time.

## Product Purpose

QuintaEarth is a hub where greentech and cleantech work is gathered, shown and traded, built on five parts: **Learn, Connect, Showcase, Solve, Trade**. The Phase 1 revenue line is "Green stories you can prove": PR and CSR storytelling where each claim links to evidence.

Success for Phase 1: about 3 paying clients by January 2027.

## Positioning

Hub + proof (confirmed 8 Oct 2026). The hub is the place; proof is the mechanism. Every story, listing and press piece is evidence-linked rather than asserted. Working line from the vault: "We help you say what you can prove and prove what you say."

## Operating Context

- Regulatory clock that drives buyer urgency: EmpCo applies from 27 Sep 2026; CBAM definitive regime from 1 Jan 2026, certificates on sale 1 Feb 2027, first declaration 30 Sep 2027; CSRD after Omnibus I covers only companies with more than 1,000 employees and more than EUR 450m turnover.
- Content taxonomy: 8 industries, 46 sub-categories (the `industries-*.html` pages and the site menu). Diksha's story library holds 230 stories (5 per sub-category, 1,200 to 1,400 words, SDG-tagged) in the Drive vault, Showcase folder.
- Recurring content: Insights essays and Press desk notes are published in dated batches.
- Community runs through a WhatsApp "Green Tech Community" group and a Telegram channel.
- Decisions live in the Drive vault "QuintaEarth-Lite" (owner Diksha). Website design was handed to Paras on 4 Oct 2026; both review design work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static multi-page HTML site (`*.html`, `styles.css`, `site.js`, `css/`, `js/`), built on the Environs template (HTML Codex, CC BY 4.0). Target hosting: GitHub Pages on quintaearth.com.
- Packages named in the vault (4 Oct): Green-Claims Proof Audit (EmpCo), CBAM Readiness & Emissions-Data Pack, Proof-Backed Impact Stories & Press Releases, Evidence-Linked Solutions Listing.
- Joining the community is free.

Open decisions (raised 8 Oct 2026, owner said "not sure yet"; do not treat as binding until confirmed):

- Whether a QuintaEarth "verified" badge or seal may appear. The vault flags EmpCo risk: such a home-made label may count as a banned sustainability label.
- Whether prices appear on the site. Current practice: none shown. Paras asked on 5 Oct for no "Ask for a proposal" button.
- Whether generic green words ("eco-friendly", "green" as filler) are banned in copy. The vault recommends avoiding them.
- How strict the minimal-copy rule is (no eyebrow labels, no filler subtitles). Paras gave this feedback on 3 Oct.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof before praise.** A claim appears only with the evidence it rests on.
2. **Two doors, one house.** Buyers and makers both land well; neither audience is a footnote to the other.
3. **Follow one field.** A visitor can pick an industry or sub-category and keep following it.
4. **Made, not performed.** Show the work and the people doing it, not slogans about sustainability.
